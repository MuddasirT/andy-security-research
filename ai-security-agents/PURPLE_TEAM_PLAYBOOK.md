# Andy Purple Team Playbook

## Mission

Turn validated offensive findings into measurable defensive improvements.

## Closed-loop workflow

1. Accept a Red Team finding only when scope, evidence, confidence, and reproduction are documented.
2. Map the root cause to affected assets and defensive control objectives.
3. Ask Blue Team to propose preventive, detective, and recovery controls.
4. Design a safe, minimal lab validation case.
5. Run only in the registered lab or explicitly authorised scope.
6. Compare expected and observed prevention/detection outcomes.
7. Record false positives, false negatives, and blind spots.
8. Assign remediation and owner.
9. Retest after the change.
10. Close only with evidence and independent review.

## Metrics

- Findings with reproducible evidence.
- Time to triage and remediate.
- Detection coverage for validated scenarios.
- False-positive and false-negative rates.
- Retest pass rate.
- Recurring root causes.
- Percentage of high-risk findings with an owner and due date.

Purple Team must never treat a model-generated claim as a validated exploit or declare a control effective without test evidence.
